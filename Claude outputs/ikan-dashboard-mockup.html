Why this tab exists
Assess holds your scores. Equip holds your programmes. Mood holds your check-ins. This is the only screen that puts all three on one axis, which is the only way a pattern between them is visible.

iKan Dashboard

The eleven widgets from src/components/screens/Dashboard.tsx, in the order that file renders them. Every colour, radius, weight and label style is a token from globals.css.

The screen itselfI built a single-programme habit tracker with a sleep diary, a breathing pacer and a streak. None of those are on your dashboard. The real one is a hub across assessments, programmes, recommendations, health data, resources and consultants
iKan ScoreThe centrepiece, and I had nothing like it. Ring, 0–100, bands at 80 Excellent / 65 Good / 45 Fair / 25 Needs Attention, colour green above 70 and amber above 40, plus trend and data-source pills

Routes, and what the id retraces

Both programme screens are keyed by the same ?id=<programId>, so one id retraces the whole enrolment: what you did, and how it ended.

/equip-program-flow?id=Doing it. Day by day
/learning-diary?id=The record of that enrolment. Program Days, Mood Journal, Assessments — all three scoped to enrollment.startedAt → completedAt
/equip-program-completion?id=How it ended. Activities, Days Active, Topics Covered, Journey Status, and what is next
/equip-resultsProposedAcross all programmes. No route sums them today

Is the Learning Diary an independent journal? No

The code answers it. LearningDiary’s empty state reads “No assessments taken during this program”, and the header renders startedAt → completedAt. It is one programme’s window over the same stores, not a second set of data.

So its Mood and Assessment tabs belong there, and this is what resolves the duplication: the global Mood Journal is every entry you have ever made; the diary’s mood tab is the same store filtered to the days you were on that programme. One store, two windows. If they ever diverge, that is a bug.

The same holds for assessments. A GAD-7 taken on day 12 of Unburn appears in Assess as part of your clinical history, and in the diary as evidence of what the programme was doing to you. Same row, two readings.

The four screens

Home is above, interactive. The other four sit in the row beneath it, independent rather than behind a tab bar, so the set reads at once.

EquipActive programme, finished programmes with their completion stats, all-time totals, and the Learning Diary entry point per programme — which exists at /learning-diary and nothing currently links to
AssessThe unfinished PHQ-9 first, then scores over time — PHQ-9 flat, GAD-7 up 4 — then history, then what you can take. The trend line is the view the app holds data for and has never drawn
Mood JournalRuns, the month coloured by how each day felt, and the entries. This is where the 30-day grid belongs, not Home
TimelineMood, sleep, programme days and assessments on one axis. The only screen where a pattern between them is visible, and the reason the tab earns its place

Notifications — the spec is what left Home

Everything moved off Home is exactly what should drive push, so the notification model is not a separate design. Three tiers, and only the first is ever pushed.

Needs you
pushed		Something is blocked or decaying and you can fix it. Partial assessment idle 48h · Health Connect unsynced 72h · mood unlogged by 21:00 · reassessment due. One a day, maximum. Silent 22:00–08:00 unless tier is high
Changed
in-app only		Score moved, tier moved, a new recommendation appeared. Worth seeing, never worth a buzz. Waits in the bell until you open the app
Happened
log		Day completed, assessment taken, mood logged. This is the old Latest Activity. It never notifies

Two rules the tiers enforce. Never push a number. "Your score dropped to 54" arrives with no action attached and lands hardest on the worst day. Never push a streak loss. "You broke an 11-day run" is a punishment notification in a burnout product.

Freshness, and the returning user

Switch the scenario above the phone. Back after 2 weeks changes four things at once, because they are one state: Health Connect goes amber with the honest line — last synced 6 days ago, your score has been running without them — the greeting changes, the current run reads 0 with your longest was 11, it is still yours, and the tier goes high, which promotes crisis support into Recommended rather than leaving it at the bottom of a long scroll.

Stale data used to render identically to fresh data. Now the values grey out, the pill goes amber, and the card says what the staleness cost.

Where the histories live

Assessment historyAssess tab. Past scores with severity, and the trend line PHQ-9 9 → GAD-7 12 that the dashboard never drew. Home only shows one when it is partial and needs finishing
Equip historyEquip tab. Completed programmes with their completion stats, and /learning-diary per programme. Home shows the live one only
Mood tracker historyMood Journal. It already owns the calendar and calculateStreak. Home keeps three numbers, the 30-day grid goes back where it came from
Latest activityNotification bell. Not the Timeline tab — I had that wrong. Home is what to do; the bell is what happened
