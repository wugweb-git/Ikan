# iKan Health — AI context

iKan Health (ikanhealth.in) is a mental wellness SPA (Specialised Programme Application) built for the Indian market. It provides structured self-care programmes, mental health assessments, mood journaling, health data tracking, and access to wellness consultants.

## What we offer

- Evidence-based wellness programmes (Equip) — stress management, sleep improvement, burnout recovery, productivity
- Mental health self-assessments (anxiety, depression, burnout, sleep, general wellbeing)
- Guided daily check-ins and mood journaling
- Integration with Android Health Connect and Google Fit for activity and sleep data
- Consultation booking with wellness professionals
