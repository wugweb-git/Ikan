Feeling worried and stressed

Gauge how overwhelmed or in control you’ve felt over the last month to understand your stress levels.

Task10 Qs
Time4 min
LevelBeginner

What is this assessment?

This assessment helps you understand how stressed or overwhelmed you’ve been feeling lately, and whether life has felt manageable or out of control.

It focuses on how you’ve been responding to everyday pressures at work, at home, or personally.

What it measures
Perceived stress and overwhelm
Sense of control vs pressure
How demanding life has felt

How it works

What to expect
  • Answer honestly
  • Takes about 5 minutes
  • Instant results
Who can take this?

This assessment is designed for adults 18+.

What you’ll see

After completing the assessment, you’ll see:

  • A summary of how frequently stress has affected you
  • Explanation of what your stress level may indicate
  • Insight into whether stress feels situational or ongoing
  • Suggested next steps such as Equip programs, resources, or further assessments
Low Stress0 – 7
Moderate Stress8 – 18
High Stress19 – 28

No labels. No judgments. Just clarity.

Things to remember

  • Answer based on the past month
  • Choose the option that fits best
  • There are no right or wrong answers
  • Go with your first instinct

Important notes

  • This is a self-help and awareness tool, not a medical diagnosis
  • Not intended for emergency or crisis situations
  • If stress feels overwhelming or unmanageable, consider reaching out to a mental health professional
  • You may choose to share your results with a professional if helpful

Sleep Tight

A gentle, guided program to help you understand and improve your sleep. There’s nothing to “fix” immediately. We’ll start by noticing patterns and building consistency.

Length31 days
A day5–10 min
PriceFree

What is this program about?

Having trouble sleeping? I-Kan’s Sleep Tight module is here. We know quality sleep is essential for your well-being. Over the next 31 days, we’ll work together to address your sleep troubles and help you achieve restful nights, improving your health, focus, and mood.

Is this for you?

Daily micro-lessons, 5–10 minutes a day.

  • Evidence-based CBT techniques
  • Progress tracking & insights
  • Personalized sleep plan
  • No pills

How it works

This program works in progressive stages.

  1. Complete your daily lessons at your own pace
  2. Track your sleep patterns and progress
  3. Practice new techniques each day
  4. Review your insights and adjust your plan

The focus is on building sustainable habits and developing practical skills you can use every day.

What you’ll gain

Better Sleep QualityFall asleep faster and wake up refreshed
Reduced Sleep AnxietyStop worrying about sleep and feel more in control
Sustainable HabitsBuild routines that last beyond the program
Improved Daily EnergyFeel more alert and energized during the day
